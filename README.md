# CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) Study Guide

A free, self-contained study guide for the **CompTIA Security+ SY0-701** exam: concise notes for all 5 domains, quick-reference sheets, and interactive practice tools.

> [!NOTE]
> Unofficial study material, not affiliated with or endorsed by CompTIA. Written with AI assistance and organized by CompTIA's published SY0-701 exam objectives. It contains **no real exam questions**. Always check the official [CompTIA Security+ exam objectives](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/security) for the current version.

## Exam at a glance

- Up to **90 questions** in **90 minutes**: multiple-choice plus performance-based questions (PBQs)
- Scored **100–900**; passing score **750**

## How to study

1. One domain note at a time, **heaviest first**: Domain 4 → 2 → 5 → 3 → 1. Answer each note's self-check out loud.
2. Ten minutes a day on [ports](study-guide/06-Reference_Ports-Protocols-Crypto.md) and [orderings & formulas](study-guide/08-Reference_Orderings-and-Formulas.md) until they're automatic.
3. Read [commonly confused pairs](study-guide/09-Commonly-Confused-Pairs.md) end to end, then use the scenario quiz and flashcards.
4. Take a timed full-length practice exam from a reputable source and restudy your two weakest domains.
5. Last 48 hours: only the [high-yield sheet](study-guide/11-Final-Review_High-Yield-Sheet.md).
